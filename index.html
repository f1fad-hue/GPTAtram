Math & model limitations

Each path compounds monthly lognormal returns: V(t+1)=V(t)×exp((μ−σ²/2)/12 + σ×Z/√12). μ is the stated portfolio net-CAGR assumption and σ is the portfolio volatility assumption. The model is educational: it does not predict returns, fully capture tails, taxes, dealing spreads, FX moves, cash distributions, or future fee changes.
